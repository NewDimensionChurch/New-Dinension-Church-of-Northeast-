Who We Are

New Dimension Church of Northeast was founded in 2008 by Rev. Algerron D. Williams Sr. to bring the Gospel and practical ministry to Northeast Columbia.

Our Mission

To worship God in spirit and in truth, make disciples of Jesus Christ, and serve our community with love.

Our Vision

New Dimension is a place where lives are changed, purpose is discovered, and God is glorified.
