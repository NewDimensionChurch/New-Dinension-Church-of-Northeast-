Official Biography

Pastor Algerron Williams Sr.

Pastor Algerron Williams Sr. is a distinguished pastor, visionary leader, and dedicated community servant whose ministry and professional career reflect a lifelong commitment to faith, leadership, and empowering others.

Having answered the call to preach the Gospel in August 1992, Pastor Williams has devoted more than 34 years to proclaiming God’s Word, strengthening the local church, and encouraging believers to discover and fulfill their God-given purpose.

He currently serves as Senior Pastor of New Dimension Church of Northeast and Zion Chapel Baptist Church of Reeder Point, both located in Columbia, South Carolina. His ministry is characterized by practical biblical teaching, inspirational preaching, leadership development, and a passion for seeing individuals and families grow spiritually.

Pastor Williams has demonstrated a longstanding commitment to denominational leadership. He currently serves as President of the Gethsemane Missionary Baptist Association, an organization representing more than 50 member churches, having previously served as its Moderator. He also serves as Vice Moderator of the Midlands Association. He has also past Moderator for the Gethsemane Baptist Association and past President of the Congress of Christian Education. Through these leadership roles, he remains committed to fostering unity, strengthening local congregations, advancing Christian education, and preparing future generations for effective ministry. In addition he has served as Player Development and Spiritual Mentor of Benedict College Men’s Basketball Team.

Recognizing the importance of theological preparation, Pastor Williams pursued studies through the Morris College, Sumter, South Carolina. He studies through Erskine Theological Seminary and Interdenominational Theological Center (ITC), Morehouse School of Religion. He remains committed to biblical scholarship, effective ministry, and lifelong learning.

As a bivocational pastor, Pastor Williams has successfully balanced his calling to ministry with a professional career spanning more than 30 plus years in the housing industry across South Carolina and North Carolina. He is a Federal licensed mortgage loan manager whose professional experience includes Branch Manager of Lending with Mungo Homes and its parent company, Berkshire Hathaway. Throughout his career, he has demonstrated a passion for educating, empowering, and assisting families in achieving the dream of homeownership.

His unique combination of pastoral leadership and housing industry expertise has allowed him to make a meaningful impact both inside and outside the walls of the church. Whether providing spiritual guidance or helping families establish financial stability through homeownership, Pastor Williams believes that leadership is ultimately about service and improving the lives of others.

Pastor Williams has been blessed to share in marriage with his wife, Jacquelyn Williams. Together, they cherish the blessings of family, including their four sons Travis, Jonathan, Algerron Jr. and Jeron Williams and six grandchildren.

Above all, Pastor Williams remains committed to serving God, strengthening the church, developing leaders, and making a lasting difference in the lives of those he is privileged to serve.

His life and leadership embody the conviction that ministry extends beyond the pulpit and that true success is measured by the lives we touch, the people we serve, and the legacy we leave behind.
