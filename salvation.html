The Good News of Jesus Christ

God loves you. The Bible teaches that all have sinned (Romans 3:23), that Jesus Christ died for our sins and rose again (1 Corinthians 15:3–4), and that salvation is God's gift of grace received through faith in Christ (Ephesians 2:8–9).

Romans 10:9 calls us to confess Jesus as Lord and believe in our hearts that God raised Him from the dead. If you are ready to respond, we would love to pray with you and help you take your next step.

Prayer of response: Lord Jesus, I believe You died for my sins and rose again. I turn to You and trust You as my Savior and Lord. Help me follow You and grow in Your Word. Amen.
The words of a prayer do not save by themselves; salvation is through faith in Jesus Christ.
